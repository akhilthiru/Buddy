# Flatmate – Product Context & Market Foundations

## 1. Product Concept

**Flatmate** is a person-first discovery and compatibility platform designed to help people find compatible flatmates.

Unlike traditional real-estate or rental marketplace platforms (e.g., MagicBricks, 99acres, NoBroker) that prioritize listing square footage and broker negotiations, Flatmate prioritizes **human compatibility, lifestyle harmony, and mutual trust**.

### The 4 Discovery Modalities
Accommodation is strictly optional. A user may:
1. **Have a room available** in an existing flat and seek a compatible flatmate to fill it.
2. **Be looking for accommodation** and seek an existing room/flat with compatible flatmates.
3. **Have accommodation but want to relocate**, seeking a new flatmate and new space.
4. **Want to find a compatible person first** to team up with before searching for an apartment together.

## 2. Core Product Pillars

### Pillar 1: Compatibility First
- Compatibility dimensions:
  - Daily sleep and wake routines (early birds vs night owls).
  - Cleanliness expectations and chore division.
  - Dietary preferences (vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan, kitchen sharing).
  - Social preferences, guest policies, noise tolerance, and party habits.
  - Financial expectations (monthly rent ceiling, shared utility split discipline).
  - Work rhythm (Work from home, hybrid, shift work).
  - Roommate gender preferences and comfort levels.

### Pillar 2: Trust and Safety
- Verified identity verification and college/workplace affiliation badges.
- Mutual opt-in matching: direct messaging unlocks ONLY after both parties like each other.
- Strict location coarsening: coordinates fuzzed to neighborhood clusters (e.g. Kazhakkoottam, Kariavattom) to prevent stalking.
- Anti-harassment reporting, one-click blocking, and automated spam mitigation.

### Pillar 3: Person-First Discovery
- Modern, engaging discovery interface inspired by dating and mutual matching mechanics.
- Rich profile cards featuring lifestyle tags, personality bios, and compatibility breakdown scores.
- Clear accommodation tags indicating whether a candidate has a room, needs a room, or is looking to team up.

## 3. Initial Target Market: Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), Kerala, India

### Geography
- **Primary Micro-Markets**:
  - **Kazhakkoottam & Kariavattom**: Heart of the Technopark tech corridor (Phase I, II, III) and University of Kerala Kariavattom campus. High density of young IT professionals and postgraduate researchers.
  - **Sreekaryam & Kulathoor**: Proximity to College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET), attracting engineering students and fresh graduates.
  - **Kowdiar, Pattom, & Sasthamangalam**: Central residential hubs with young working professionals in government, media, healthcare, and consulting.

### Demographics & Personas
1. **The Technopark IT Professional**:
   - Age 22–30, working in software development or BPO.
   - Values quiet sleep hours, reliable high-speed Wi-Fi, cleanliness, and proximity to Technopark campus.
2. **The College Student**:
   - Age 18–24, studying at CET, SCT, or University of Kerala.
   - Budget-conscious (₹3,000–₹6,000/month), seeking study-compatible flatmates with shared cooking or tiffin arrangements.
3. **The Relocating Professional**:
   - Moving from Kochi, Bengaluru, or Chennai to Trivandrum. Needs trusted, safe flatmates without paying broker fees.

## 4. Platform Strategy
- **Phase 1 (Initial)**: Mobile-First Responsive Web Application optimized for mobile browsers.
- **Phase 2**: Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline caching and native-like home screen installation.
- **Phase 3**: Cross-platform native mobile applications (React Native / Flutter) once PMF is achieved.
