# Market Research Skill

## Purpose
Gather, structure, and analyze market data on flatmate searching behavior, rental dynamics, competitor offerings, and regulatory/cultural factors in the target launch geography (Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India).

## When to Use It
- During Workflow A (Ideation) and market expansion planning.
- When benchmarking against local property brokers, classifieds, and rental platforms.
- To validate pricing, monetization, and user acquisition strategies.

## Required Inputs
- Target launch geography: Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India.
- Target demographics: Students (CET, SCT, University of Kerala, Medical College) and working professionals (Technopark Phase I, II, III).
- Current competitor data (NoBroker, Nestaway, Facebook Groups, Olx, broker networks).

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Analyze Competitive Landscape**: Audit existing platforms across discovery models, pricing, safety/verification, and person-first matching capabilities.
2. **Profile Demographic Segments**: Model income levels, monthly rental budgets (e.g. ₹3,000 - ₹12,000/month), mobility patterns, and preferred residential clusters (Kazhakkoottam, Kariavattom, Sreekaryam, Kowdiar, Pattom).
3. **Analyze Cultural & Social Factors**: Document local roommate preferences regarding dietary habits (veg/non-veg), guest policies, working shifts (night shifts vs regular), and gender preferences.
4. **Identify Strategic Moats**: Articulate why property-first platforms fail at lifestyle compatibility and how Flatmate differentiates.
5. **Compile Market Report**: Publish comprehensive research in `docs/product/market-research.md`.

## Expected Outputs
- `docs/product/market-research.md`
- Competitor differentiation matrix in `docs/product/competitor-analysis.md`.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Explicit analysis of both student and IT professional sub-segments.
- [ ] Local geographic specificity (key micro-markets in Trivandrum).
- [ ] Concrete differentiation showing why Flatmate is not just another rental portal.
